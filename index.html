Why Is Fubo TV Not Working?

Fubo TV may stop working because of several common issues, including an unstable internet connection, outdated applications, browser problems, account login errors, device compatibility issues, service interruptions, or incorrect account settings. Before contacting support, try basic troubleshooting steps such as restarting your device, checking your internet connection, updating the Fubo TV app, and signing out and back into your account.

Common Fubo TV Problems

Login Problems

Unable to sign in to your Fubo TV account? Check your email address and password, reset your password if necessary, and make sure your internet connection is working correctly.

Streaming Issues

If Fubo TV is not streaming properly, check your internet speed, restart the streaming device, close unnecessary applications, and try playing another channel or program.

Buffering Problems

Frequent buffering can be caused by a slow or unstable internet connection. Restart your router, move closer to your Wi-Fi source, or try a wired connection when available.

App Not Working

If the Fubo TV app crashes or does not open, restart the device, update the app, clear the application cache where supported, or reinstall the latest version.

Account Issues

Account access problems may involve incorrect login information, password issues, subscription status, or account-related settings.

Device Problems

Check whether your streaming device, smart TV, mobile device, or browser is supported and updated. Restarting the device can also resolve temporary errors.

Quick Troubleshooting Steps

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Restart your Wi-Fi router or modem.
  • Close and reopen the Fubo TV app.
  • Restart your smart TV, phone, computer, or streaming device.
  • Check that you are using the correct Fubo TV login credentials.
  • Reset your password if you cannot access your account.
  • Update the Fubo TV application to the latest available version.
  • Clear the browser cache if Fubo TV is not working in a web browser.
  • Try another supported device or browser.
  • Check whether the problem affects only one channel or all content.

Fubo TV Login Troubleshooting

If you cannot log in to Fubo TV, first verify that the email address and password you entered are correct. If you have forgotten your password, use the password reset option associated with your account. Make sure your browser or Fubo TV application is updated and that your internet connection is stable.

If the login page continues to display an error, try signing in from another supported browser or device. Avoid repeatedly entering incorrect credentials and make sure you are accessing the legitimate Fubo TV service.

Fubo TV Streaming Troubleshooting

When Fubo TV is not streaming, start by testing your internet connection with another website or application. If other services are also slow, restart your router and modem. If your internet connection is working normally, restart the Fubo TV application and your streaming device.

You can also try switching to another channel or program. If only one piece of content is affected, the problem may be limited to that particular stream. Keeping your application and device software updated can also help prevent compatibility and playback problems.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Fubo TV not working?

Fubo TV may not work because of internet connectivity problems, application errors, outdated software, account issues, device compatibility problems, or temporary service interruptions. Restarting the app and device is a good first step.

Why can't I log in to my Fubo TV account?

Check that your email address and password are correct. If you forgot your password, use the password recovery option. You can also try another supported browser or device.

How can I fix Fubo TV buffering?

Check your internet connection, restart your router, close other applications using bandwidth, and restart your streaming device. A wired connection may also provide a more stable connection when available.

Why is my Fubo TV app crashing?

Restart the application and device, check for application updates, and reinstall the app if necessary. Make sure your device meets the current requirements for using the service.

What should I do if Fubo TV works on one device but not another?

Check the affected device for software updates, restart it, and verify that the Fubo TV application is supported. Reinstalling the application may also resolve device-specific problems.

How can I get additional help with Fubo TV?

If basic troubleshooting does not resolve the problem, gather your account information and a description of the error before seeking additional technical assistance.
